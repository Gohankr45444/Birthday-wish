# Magical Hinglish Birthday Experience v2.0 (Full Interactive Edition) 🎂✨

## Included Interactive Mechanics
1. **Touch Sparkle Trail & Haptics**: Every finger drag spawns stardust particles + triggers subtle mobile haptics.
2. **Constellation Lock (Scene 1)**: Connect 5 glowing stars in the night sky to unlock the birthday message.
3. **3D Polaroid Flip & Double-Tap Hearts (Scene 3)**: Double-tap photos for floating hearts or flip the card 180° for a secret Hinglish roast.
4. **3-Stage 3D Cake Mini-Game (Scene 4)**:
   - **Stage 1 (Light)**: Tap the 3D candle wicks (or sparkler button) to light all 5 flames.
   - **Stage 2 (Blow)**: Blow into the phone microphone (Web Audio DSP) to bend and extinguish the flames.
   - **Stage 3 (Slice)**: Swipe across the 3D cake to slice out a piece!
5. **Wax Seal & Foil Scratch-Off Gift Card (Scene 6)**: Crack the gold wax seal to unfold the letter, then rub the golden foil card to reveal a secret birthday voucher.
6. **Poppable 3D Finale Balloons (Scene 7)**: Tap floating 3D balloons to pop them and spawn Hinglish compliment badges.

## Quick Start
1. `npm install`
2. `npm run dev`
3. Edit `src/content.ts` to customize all names, inside jokes, secret notes, voucher prize, and photos.
